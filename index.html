Questions

What is Polaris?

A set of computer-generated characters built on Ethereum. Each form is produced by the contract rather than drawn by hand or served from an image host.

Is the artwork actually onchain?

The character is assembled by the contract and returned by the token metadata call. There is no separate image server that has to stay alive for your character to render.

When is the mint, and what does it cost?

Timing, supply and price are published on @polarisforms before anything opens. Nothing on this page is an offer or a promise of value.

Can I use my character elsewhere?

Holder licence terms are in the Terms of Use. Anything the licence does not grant stays with the project.

Where do announcements come from?

X is the only official channel. If an announcement does not come from @polarisforms, it is not ours.
